Plane artwork acceptance gallery

This unlinked surface reads the production v1 manifest and assets directly. Missing files are expected for planned records; artwork is never approved or published by this page.

Reject when any view shows

  • Wrong direction or backwards primary wordmark
  • Misspelled or unrecognizable main airline name
  • Malformed wings, tail, engines, rotors, propellers, or gear
  • Engine or rotor count contradicting the assigned family
  • Inconsistent angle, extreme scale mismatch, bad crop, or broken baseline
  • Clipping, opaque corners, matte halos, or severe color fringing
  • Watermark or unrelated scenery
  • Poor hero recognition or an ugly blip silhouette

Do not reject solely for tiny plausible incidental lettering, minor panel-detail inventions, or small natural differences between directions.

Review method

Compare the independently generated right and left pair. Inspect all three tilts at 272 px hero and 46 px blip size on the production sky and checkerboard. Record the decision and reason in the pair notes.
